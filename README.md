# BD BO Account Tax Rebate Calculator (PWA & APK Ready)

Bangladesh Income Tax Act compliant calculator to determine eligible share market investment for Tax Rebate, configured with full offline Service Worker and Web App Manifest for Android APK generation.

## How to Generate Android APK (No Coding Required)
1. Upload all 9 files from this folder to your GitHub repository and deploy on **Vercel**.
2. Copy your live Vercel URL (e.g., `https://your-app.vercel.app`).
3. Go to [PWABuilder.com](https://www.pwabuilder.com), paste your URL, and click **Start**.
4. Click **Package for Stores** → **Android** → **Generate Package** to download your installable `.apk` file.
